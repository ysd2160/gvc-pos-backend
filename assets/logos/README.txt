Yahan apna logo daalo (PNG format), file ka naam bilkul aisa hi rakho:

  logo.png     -> Good Vibes Cafe

Tips:
- Kam se kam 300px chaudai. Transparent background theek hai.
- Thermal printer sirf black & white print karta hai, isliye simple aur high-contrast logo (dark design, saaf ya white background) sabse acha aata hai. Rangeen logo automatically black/white mein badal jayega.
- Logo na ho to bill bina logo ke banega - kuch tootega nahi.
